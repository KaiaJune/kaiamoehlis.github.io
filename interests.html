Energy, Sustainability, and Data

One of my primary interests is the role that data plays in improving energy systems and supporting the transition toward renewable energy. As countries move toward cleaner sources of electricity such as wind and solar power, understanding how energy markets operate becomes increasingly important. Through data analysis and modeling, researchers and analysts can identify patterns that help improve reliability, efficiency, and sustainability across energy systems.

I am particularly interested in analyzing environmental and energy datasets to better understand how different factors influence power markets and environmental outcomes. Working with data allows researchers to uncover trends, evaluate policy impacts, and support decisions that shape the future of energy infrastructure and environmental protection.

In addition to my academic interests, I enjoy exploring new places and learning about different cultures through travel. Visiting new environments provides valuable perspective on sustainability challenges and how different communities approach environmental conservation and development.

Topics I Enjoy Exploring

  • Renewable energy systems
  • Energy market analysis
  • Environmental data science
  • Sustainability and climate policy
  • Travel and cultural exploration
