About Me

I am a rising energy market analyst passionate about using data to drive meaningful insights and support the transition toward renewable energy. My experience at Wood Mackenzie involved leading analytical projects on U.S. electricity markets, developing Python-based workflows for forecasting, model validation, and risk assessment. This work strengthened my skills in quantitative analysis, data integrity, and translating complex results into actionable insights for clients and internal stakeholders.

During my time at the Institute for Energy Efficiency at UCSB, I analyzed ESG metrics for data centers, validated large datasets, and supported downstream reporting, including congressional briefings. I gained a deep appreciation for compliance-focused reporting, structured workflows, and delivering high-quality results under tight deadlines.

I enjoy combining creativity and technical problem-solving, whether it’s filling data gaps in market models using geographic data or streamlining analytical workflows. My work supports accurate performance monitoring, better market forecasts, and improved decision-making across teams.

Outside of work, I enjoy traveling, exploring new cultures, and cooking. These experiences inspire my interest in the intersection of energy, data, and global environmental stewardship.
