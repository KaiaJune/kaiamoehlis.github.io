About Me

Hi! My name is Kaia Moehlis and I am a rising energy market analyst with experience applying programming-based methods to analyze complex energy, environmental, and market datasets. I focus on transforming large datasets into clear, decision-relevant insights through statistical, economic, and spatial analysis. My work reflects strong analytical rigor, intellectual curiosity, and a commitment to collaborative problem solving in both research and professional environments.

I am currently studying Environmental and Sustainability Sciences with a minor in Data Science at Northeastern University. Through my academic coursework and professional experiences, I have developed strong skills in data analysis, programming, and research methods. I am particularly interested in understanding how data can be used to improve energy systems and support the transition toward renewable energy.

My experience includes working with large environmental and energy datasets, analyzing power market trends, and developing programming workflows that help translate complex data into clear insights. I enjoy working at the intersection of sustainability, environmental systems, and analytical problem solving, and I hope to continue contributing to projects that advance clean energy and sustainable decision making.
